Sudo Liquid Glass - live, real Liquid Glass for Photoshop and Illustrator.

PHOTOSHOP (Windows and Mac) - pick one
  1. Double-click SudoLiquidGlass.ccx. Creative Cloud installs it.
  2. Windows: double-click "Install Photoshop on Windows.bat".
     Mac: double-click "Install Photoshop on Mac.command". If macOS says it can't be opened,
     right-click it > Open > Open (it is a small script, not signed by Apple).
  Then restart Photoshop and open Plugins > Sudo Liquid Glass.
  Needs Photoshop 2023 (v24.2) or newer and the Creative Cloud desktop app.

ILLUSTRATOR (Windows only for now)
  1. Close Illustrator.
  2. Double-click "Install Illustrator on Windows.bat" and allow it to make changes
     (it copies LiquidGlass.aip into Illustrator's Plug-ins folder).
  3. Start Illustrator, select a shape and choose Effect > Stylize > Liquid Glass...
     Change it later: double-click Liquid Glass in the Appearance panel, or Object > Edit Liquid Glass...
  Needs Illustrator 2026 on Windows 10 or 11 (with the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, built into Windows 11).
  There is no Mac Illustrator version yet.

Why .ccx and not .zxp? ZXP is the format of Adobe's older CEP extensions.
The Photoshop plugin is a modern UXP plugin; Adobe packages those as .ccx.
