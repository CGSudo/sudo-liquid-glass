Sudo Liquid Glass - live, real Liquid Glass for Photoshop and Illustrator.

PHOTOSHOP (Windows and Mac) - pick one
  1. Double-click SudoLiquidGlass.ccx. Creative Cloud installs it.
  2. Windows: double-click "Install Photoshop on Windows.bat".
     Mac: double-click "Install Photoshop on Mac.command". If macOS says it can't be opened,
     right-click it > Open > Open (it is a small script, not signed by Apple).
  Then restart Photoshop and open Plugins > Sudo Liquid Glass.
  Needs Photoshop 2023 (v24.2) or newer and the Creative Cloud desktop app.

ILLUSTRATOR (Windows and Mac)
  1. Close Illustrator.
  2. Windows: double-click "Install Illustrator on Windows.bat" and allow it to make changes.
     Mac: double-click "Install Illustrator on Mac.command" (right-click > Open if macOS blocks it;
     it asks for your password to copy into the Illustrator folder).
     Both install the plug-in (LiquidGlass.aip) and the panel (SudoLiquidGlassPanel .zxp).
  3. Start Illustrator and open Window > Extensions > Sudo Liquid Glass.
     Select shapes or text and click Make Glass. Select a glass to change it.
  Needs Illustrator 2026. The Mac version hasn't been tried on a real Mac yet.

Why .ccx and .zxp? Photoshop's plugin is UXP (.ccx). Illustrator's panel is CEP (.zxp),
because Illustrator doesn't run UXP plugins yet.
